# Provenance and attribution

All source code in this repository was written by the authors of the manuscript.
No third-party source code, publisher PDF, or external asset is redistributed
here.

The comparison methods are the authors' **own reimplementations** of published
algorithms, all driven through one shared interface by `Experiment_Statistic.py`.
The original publications are cited in the manuscript and listed below for
orientation; the corresponding entries in the manuscript's reference list carry
the full bibliographic details.

|---|---|---|
| `Previous_Algorithm/HERO.py` | HERO | Mi et al. (2011) |
| `Previous_Algorithm/SIDR.py` | SIDR | Chen et al. (2020) |
| `Previous_Algorithm/Centering.py` | global-position centring rule | specification in Methods |
| `Previous_Algorithm/CR_MGC.py` | CR-MGC, graph-optimisation-style recovery | specification in Methods |

## Two notes on naming and cohorts

- **Ablation cohorts.** `Logs/manuscript_revision/ablation.csv` holds the
  offset-zero cohort (50 configurations per variant) and
  `ablation_repair.csv` holds a retained, separately recorded cohort (50 further
  configurations per variant) that is merged into the N=1000 ablation
  statistics. `tools/repair_ablation_provenance.py` re-derives and validates the
  damage provenance of the second cohort; the shipped tables are built from both.
- **`case129` / `connected_ring`.** `record_case129.py`,
  `Logs/manuscript_revision/diagnostics/case129_replay.*` and the
  `Paper/figs/fig3_cache/` ring caches carry internal identifiers from the
  historical sweep. They are diagnostic artefacts used by the operator-ablation
  and ring-centre analyses; the identifiers are historical labels, not
  quantities reported in the manuscript.

## Difference from the working repository

`tools/build_manuscript_supplement.py` carries one guard that the working copy
does not need: when `Paper/sn-article.tex` is absent it writes
`Paper/tables/*.csv` and `supplement_audit.json` and skips the inline insertion
into the manuscript, so the tables can be rebuilt from this release alone.
